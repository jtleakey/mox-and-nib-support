FAQ

How do I play?

Tap the left half of the screen to make Mox hop and the right half to make Nib hop. Clear the obstacles in both worlds.

How do I pause or mute?

Use the buttons at the top of the screen.

Does the game collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
