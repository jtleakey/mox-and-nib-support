FrameDelta Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

FrameDelta is an app by James Leakey. It keeps a dated photo record of a space on your device.

The short version: FrameDelta does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection to an app service. Photos, dates, and notes stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. FrameDelta does not send your photos, notes, reports, or any information about you or your device to James Leakey or to anyone else. The app has no analytics or tracking, and it does not include third-party advertising or analytics tools.

What stays on your device

You choose photos with Apple’s system photo picker, or take one with the camera. FrameDelta stores a display copy in the app’s documents on your device, along with the dates and the notes you write. It does not read your whole photo library, and it does not copy location metadata into that stored copy. The original photo stays in Photos.

Lining up two photos uses Apple’s Vision framework on this device. Nothing about the photos is sent to a server. Notes are only what you type. FrameDelta does not describe the photos or write notes for you.

A dated report is a PDF you share yourself. Saving an annotated image or a divider image to Photos asks for add-only permission, and only when you choose that.

Device backups may include the app’s documents, according to your device and backup settings. Photos you save or PDFs you share follow your Photos, iCloud, and share-sheet settings. Deleting the app deletes the records stored in it.

Permissions

Camera permission is requested only if you take a photo in the app. Adding a photo from your library uses the system photo picker and does not require full library access. Permission to add an image to Photos is requested only when you choose to save one.

Children

FrameDelta does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

If you buy FrameDelta through Apple’s App Store, Apple handles the payment. We do not receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers that purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. The App Store privacy disclosures should be reviewed at the same time.
