How to use FrameDelta

Add a place

A place is a named record you will photograph again. Name it something you’ll recognize when you come back, such as a kitchen remodel.

Add photos

Add a photo from your library with Apple’s photo picker, or take one with the camera. FrameDelta uses the capture date when the photo has one; otherwise it uses the day you added it. You can edit the date.

Compare and line up

Open a pair of photos — by default, the oldest and the newest. Move the divider to compare them. Align lines the later photo up with the earlier one on your device. You can also pan and zoom by hand, or return to the original framing.

Notes and reports

In Inspect, tap a detail to add a numbered note in your own words. FrameDelta does not describe the photos or write notes for you. Report builds a dated PDF you can share, including both photos, your notes, and the date it was prepared.

For App Review

To try the app without personal photos, tap Review with sample photos on the home screen. That opens a sample kitchen remodel with two notes already in place. The sample is labeled as sample imagery in the app and on the report.

Privacy

FrameDelta keeps the record on your device. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. See the privacy policy.
