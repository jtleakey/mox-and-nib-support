Privacy Policy for Mox & Nib

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Mox & Nib is a game made by James Leakey. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version: Mox & Nib does not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Mox & Nib has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score and your sound setting on your own device so they are there next time you play. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

Mox & Nib does not include any third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Children

Mox & Nib does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

Mox & Nib is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we do not receive your payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy covers that purchase.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email jtleakey@outlook.com.
